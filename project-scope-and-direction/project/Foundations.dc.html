04

AI visual language

One system, five roles. No sparkles, no robots, no rainbow gradients.
The diamond
The diamond marks AI and appears only where AI did the work. It's always followed by a mono role label.
Dashed = generated
AI content sits in a dashed cyan frame, which says "check this." Once a person approves it or an action runs, the frame turns solid.
my_locationAlways show context
Every AI surface lists what it's "Using" — the lesson, timestamp, file or task. You can remove items from that list.
schoolHint before answer
Learning AI starts with a nudge, then a hint, then an example. You have to ask for the full solution.
